# Liaqat Brothers CRM — Staff quick start (30 minutes of training)

For the training session: sit with each person at their own phone/computer and make them do the exercises themselves. Roles: **Staff** (daily work), **Manager** (approvals, corrections, reports), **Owner** (everything, users, settings).

## 1. Sign in
Open the web address, enter your email and password. New people register first; the owner approves the account. If you see "account not active", ask the owner.

## 2. Find or add a client (never add twice)
1. Tap the **magnifier** (top right on phone) or press **Ctrl+K** on a computer. Type a name, phone number, CNIC or car registration.
2. Found? Open it. Not found? **Clients > Add client**. If a yellow "possible duplicate" box appears, open that client instead.
3. On the client screen you can **Call** or **WhatsApp** with one tap. Do not retype the number.

## 3. Make a quotation
**Fee Calculator** → choose client and vehicle → check the fees → **Save quotation**. Wait for the spinner to finish; do not tap twice. Download the PDF or send it on WhatsApp from the quotation screen.

## 4. Quotation → case
1. Open the quotation. Staff can move it **Draft → Pending → Approved** (and Rejected). Going backwards needs a **Manager** and a reason.
2. Approved quotation: tap **Create case**. One case only; tapping again opens the same case.
3. In the case, tick each document when the client hands it over. Move the stage: Documents → Submitted → In process → Ready → Delivered. Add a note when something happens.

## 5. Payments
- Open the quotation (or the client's Ledger) → **Record payment** → amount, date, mode (cash / bank / cheque) → Save. You cannot edit the amount afterwards.
- Wrong entry? Ask a **Manager/Owner** to **Reverse** it with a reason; then enter it again correctly.
- A showroom paying for many cars: record **payment on account** on the showroom's Ledger, then allocate it to quotations.

## 6. Reminders (follow-ups)
**Reminders** (bell icon) → New. Link it to a client or case, choose date/time (Pakistan time). When done tap **Done**; if the client asks for later, **Snooze**. The bell shows what is due.

## 7. Delivery
When the vehicle/file is handed over: case stage **Delivered**, status **Completed**. A Manager completes the quotation. After that staff cannot reopen it.

## 8. If something goes wrong
| Message | Meaning | You do |
|---|---|---|
| "No internet… nothing was saved" | Connection dropped | Check connection, repeat the step |
| "Session has expired" | Logged out | Sign in again, repeat |
| "You do not have permission" | Your role cannot do this | Ask a Manager or the Owner |
| "This record already exists" | Duplicate | Search and open the existing one |
| Anything else | — | Take a screenshot with the time and tell the owner |

## 9. Rules of the shop that the system enforces
Staff cannot see company money totals, reports, audit log or settings. Payments cannot be edited, only reversed by a manager. Deleted/archived clients with history stay in the records.
